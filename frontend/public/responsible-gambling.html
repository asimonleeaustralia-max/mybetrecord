Responsible gambling

Last updated: July 2026

mybetrecord is a private ledger for recording bets you have already placed elsewhere. We do not accept or facilitate wagers, provide tips, sell odds, or advertise bookmakers.

Betting should be entertainment, not income. If gambling stops being fun, pause and seek help.

Support resources

  • BeGambleAware
  • Gambling Therapy
  • Gambler’s Help (Australia)
  • National Council on Problem Gambling (US)

Our commitments

  • Adults only (18+).
  • No in-app bookmaker links or gambling advertisements.
  • Educational / record-keeping content only — not betting or financial advice.
